**Situation:** The team was growing from 5 to 9 people, and new hires were taking two months to make their first real contribution.

**Task:** Make onboarding repeatable without pulling senior engineers off their work.

**Action:** I wrote a two-week starter plan with a real first task, paired each hire with a rotating buddy, and kept a running FAQ from every question asked.

**Result:** Time to first merged change dropped from about 10 weeks to 3 for the next four hires.
